01 · About

What I do

I am a Senior Engineer specializing in cloud security, application security, and DevSecOps, currently working at Innov8 Pvt Ltd. My work centers on hands-on implementation of enterprise security solutions — from securing cloud environments and workloads to embedding security controls into software development and CI/CD workflows.

Day to day, I perform application security assessments using SAST and DAST methodologies, implement and administer security platforms such as Veracode, Prisma Cloud, and Zimperium, and work with development, infrastructure, and security teams to establish practical secure development practices. I care about security that enables delivery rather than blocking it.

Before my current role, I worked in IT Risk and Security Operations at Allianz Insurance Lanka Limited, gaining experience in a regulated financial services environment.
